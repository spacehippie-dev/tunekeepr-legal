TuneKeepr

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

TuneKeepr is an iOS app for DJs that extracts tracks from screenshots and organizes them into a crate system. This policy explains what data TuneKeepr handles, where it goes, and what choices you have.

What TuneKeepr Does Not Do

TuneKeepr does not collect analytics or usage data. It does not display advertising. It does not sell, share, or rent your personal information. It does not create user accounts or require registration. It does not track your location. The developer of TuneKeepr operates no servers and never receives your data.

Data Stored on Your Device

All track data you create in TuneKeepr — titles, artists, tags, and crate organization — is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This data is not transmitted to any server and is not accessible to TuneKeepr's developer. Deleting the app removes this data.

If you add an Anthropic API key, it is stored in your device's Keychain, which is encrypted by iOS. You can remove it at any time with Settings → Clear API Key. Because iOS may keep Keychain items after an app is deleted, clear your key before deleting the app if you want it removed.

Track Extraction

TuneKeepr reads tracklists from screenshots you select from your photo library. Your screenshots never leave your device.

  • Standard extraction (default): Apple's Vision framework reads the text in your screenshots, and TuneKeepr parses it into tracks entirely on your device. On supported devices, Apple Intelligence may refine uncertain results, also on-device. Nothing is sent off your device.
  • AI extraction with Claude (optional): If you add your own Anthropic API key in Settings and tap Extract Tracks Using AI, the text read from your screenshots (not the images) is sent directly from your device to Anthropic's Claude API to identify artists, titles, and tags. TuneKeepr asks for your permission before the first time this happens. This text is processed by Anthropic under its privacy policy and the terms of your Anthropic account. The developer of TuneKeepr never has access to your API key or to the text you send.

Apple Music Integration

If you choose to use Apple Music, TuneKeepr uses Apple's MusicKit framework. Authorization is handled by iOS through a system dialog. TuneKeepr searches the Apple Music catalog for your tracks and adds the ones you choose to your library. It does not read your existing library or listening history.

Third-Party Services

TuneKeepr communicates with the following services, only when you use the related feature:

  • Anthropic (Claude API) — receives extracted screenshot text when you use AI extraction. Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Apple MusicKit — receives search queries and library add requests when you send tracks to Apple Music. Apple's privacy policy.

No data is sent to any other third-party service.

Deleting Your Data

Use Settings → Clear All Tracks to delete your saved tracks, and Settings → Clear API Key to remove your Anthropic key. Deleting the app removes your tracks and settings.

Children's Privacy

TuneKeepr is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated — for example, when new streaming services are added — the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date.
